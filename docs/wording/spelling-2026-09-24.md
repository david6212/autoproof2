## The three patterns

**1. A count is interpolated into a sentence that only has a plural form.**
This is the single biggest source of "the app doesn't sound Hebrew". It is not ignorance:
the codebase already writes the singular branch correctly in **twelve** places —
`טיפול אחד`, `רשומה אחת`, `תמונה אחת`, `ביקור אחד`, `קריאת שירות פתוחה`,
`אדם אחד דיווח`, `דיווח אחד`, `אזור אחד הושחר`, `חודש אחד`. It is simply missing in
**28 others**, so the same app that says "רשומה אחת שתועדה על ידי הבעלים" on the car page
says "לרכב יש 1 רשומות טיפול" in the delete dialog. Every relative-time helper in the app
(`_ago`, `_timeAgo`, `ageLabel`) has this hole at the 1-hour and 1-day boundary.

**2. The app has not decided whether it addresses one person or several.**
`נסה שוב` appears **20** times, `נסו שוב` **21** times. **36 files mix both forms**, and the
worst case is the login flow, where the screen's own errors are plural
(`login_screen.dart:79-91` — `נסו שוב`, `בדקו ונסו שוב`) while the errors thrown underneath
it by `auth_repository.dart:184-207` are singular (`נסה שוב`, `בדוק את הספרות`) — a user
who mistypes a code sees both registers in one session. The same split runs through
`claim_vehicle_screen` (`הזינו את קוד המסירה` above the button `בדוק קוד`),
`profile_screen` (`הפרופיל שלך` / `מחקו את החשבון`), `write_review_screen`
(`תודה שעזרת לקהילה` / `נסו שוב`), `notifications_screen` (`ההתראות שלך` / `מה נציג לכם`)
and `car_notes_section` (`היה הראשון לשתף מה מצאת` / `מה מצאתם בבדיקה?`).
Buttons add a third register on top: singular imperative (`שמור`, `פרסם`, `הוסף`), plural
imperative (`מחקו את החשבון`, `בחרו רכבים`), and gerund (`הוספת מקום`, `רענון נתוני המרשם`).

**3. Every typographic convention in the app exists twice, split by feature area.**
The marketplace side writes `₪132,000`; the garage/fuel side writes `1,200 ₪` — thirteen
sites against twelve. Loading states are `שומר...` on one screen and `שומר…` on another.
`ק"מ` is ASCII in 41 places and `ק״מ` (gershayim) in one. `צ׳אט` is geresh in three files
and `צ'אט` in three others. `מד אוץ`, `מד-אוץ`, `מד האוץ`. `14 יום` and `14 ימים`.
`שינוי מבנה` and `שינוי מבני`. Nothing here is misspelt; everything is spelt twice, and a
reader who moves between two screens notices it as sloppiness even without naming it.
Worth knowing: **exactly one string in `lib/` uses bidi isolation marks**
(`active_warnings_section.dart:145`). Every other mixed Hebrew/number/₪ line trusts the
renderer.

## What now holds the conventions in place

`test/wording_conventions_test.dart` — 17 tests over the whole of `lib/`, with comments
stripped so a comment that quotes a banned string is not read as an offence:

- **plural address** — seven singular forms banned anywhere in a string (`נסה`, `בדוק`,
  `הזן`, `ודא`, `התחבר`, `חזור`, `אינך`), twenty-six more banned where a string literal
  *opens* with them (a button label), and the singular possessives and past tenses
  (`שלך`, `אליך`, `עליך`, `שהזנת`, `שבחרת`, …). Words that also read as a noun or an
  adjective — `שמור` in `עותק שמור`, `הגב` — are only caught in the label position, which
  is why the check does not fire on legitimate Hebrew;
- **the ellipsis** — no Hebrew string may trail off in three periods;
- **the apostrophe** — no U+05F3/U+05F4 anywhere (`place.dart`'s search normaliser is
  exempt: it strips *both* forms on purpose, because registry names arrive either way);
- **money** — no `₪` immediately before a number, and no `₪` glued onto an interpolation;
  plus the formatter's own output;
- **the phone number** — E.164 may not reach a rendered string;
- **count agreement** — an interpolated count in front of one of fifteen plural nouns has
  to carry a `== 1` branch within the surrounding lines.

It was mutation-checked: seven planted violations produced seven failures.

Three exemptions are recorded in the file with their reason: `place_detail_screen`,
`market_price_band` and `publish_from_vehicle_screen` each interpolate a count that cannot
be 1 (`Place.minRatingsToShow` is 3, `MarketStats.minSample` is 8, and the "תיק מתועד"
sentence only renders when `badged`), so a singular branch there would be unreachable code.
